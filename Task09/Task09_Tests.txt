Note: this simple program treats a lower current reading as a rollover. A real billing system should check whether the reading is a plausible rollover.

Alternative arrays: tariff limits/rates in arrays with s slabs take O(s) time per bill and O(s) space for the table. Fixed slab statements take O(1) time and extra space. Arrays are easier to update when the number of slabs changes.
